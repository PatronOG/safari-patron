Critter list sprites
===================

Source: Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki contributors
https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Critter_Safari

37 sprite thumbnails are bundled in assets/safari-patron/textures/critters/.
They are packaged locally; using this feature makes no requests to the wiki.
Individual file pages and exact thumbnail URLs are listed in Critter-Icon-Sources.json.
All thumbnails are unchanged except Duplico: the first GIF frame was converted to PNG.
